QR Scanner (“the app”) is designed to work on your device without collecting your personal data. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

1Camera

The app uses your camera only to detect QR codes and barcodes. Camera images are processed on your device in real time. They are not saved, uploaded or shared.

2Photos

If you choose “Gallery”, you select one image using your phone's system photo picker, and the app reads the code from that image on your device. The app does not get access to the rest of your photo library.

3Scan history and settings

Your scan results, favorites and settings are stored only on your device. You can:

  • turn off history in Settings
  • delete individual scans
  • clear your whole history at any time

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

4Links and other actions

The app never opens a link on its own unless you turn on “Open safe links automatically”. It warns you about suspicious links before you open them. When you tap an action such as Open link, Call, Send message or Compose email, the content is passed to the matching app on your phone. That app has its own privacy policy.

5QR codes you create

QR codes you create are generated on your device. If you share one, it is sent only to the app you choose.

6App updates

On Android, the app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available. This is handled by Google Play and is covered by Google's privacy policy.

7Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no tracking.

8Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

9Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown in the app in a new release, and the “Last updated” date above will change.

10Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue at github.com/devcoreapps/QRCodeScanner/issues.
